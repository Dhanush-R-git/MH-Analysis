# Helplines in India for Suicide Prevention

Below is a curated list of free, confidential helplines and crisis support services available across India. Each entry includes key details such as operating hours, contact methods, and the types of support provided.

---

## 1. 1 Life (Crisis Support & Suicide Prevention)

* **Audience:** Everybody
* **Description:** Support for those experiencing severe stress, suicidal thoughts, depression, or confusion.
* **Availability:** 24/7 (Open)
* **Staff:** Counselors & Volunteers
* **Contact:**

  * **Phone:** [78930-78930](tel:78930-78930)
  * **Website:** [1life.org.in](https://1life.org.in/)
* **Mode:** Call
