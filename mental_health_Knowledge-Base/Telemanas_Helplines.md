# Tele MANAS: National Tele-Mental Health Helpline

Tele MANAS is a comprehensive mental health care service under the National Tele Mental Health Programme of India.

## Helpline Numbers

Dial our toll-free numbers to connect with a counsellor:

* **14416**
* **1-800-891-4416**

## Mobile App

Access mental health resources and support on your smartphone:

* [Get it on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=org.telemanas.citizen&pcampaignid=web_share)
* [Get it on App Store](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/tele-manas/id6738463055)
